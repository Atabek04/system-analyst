## The interlocked decision chain

These are NOT independent choices. Dependency order:

```
Price point  →  partner's absolute ₽ per sale
   ↓                    ↓
who funds ads  →  whether 30% is generous or starving
   ↓                    ↓
job guarantee  →  raises price you can charge BUT adds refund liability
   ↓                    ↓
installment    →  the "headline number" buyers actually compare
   ↓
ALL of it  →  feeds back into the 35→25% partner ratchet
```

## Decision A — Pricing model

**Choice: Hybrid — flagship program (hero) + one cheap front-door mini-course.**

| Model | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| Full program only | Max LTV; matches "cohesive journey" moat; higher partner ₽/sale | Highest trust barrier for unknown brand; big CAC |
| Modular per-topic | Low entry → easy first yes; matches systemanalyst.life band | **Fragments students = adopts competitor's weakness**; low LTV; partner ₽/sale too small to fund ads |
| **Hybrid (chosen)** | Trust ladder without fragmentation; mini-course = paid lead magnet + email list; flagship captures LTV | Two products to market; risk students stop at mini-course |
| Subscription | Predictable MRR | Wrong fit — SA training is finish-and-leave, not ongoing use; **kill it** |

Front-door mini-course is positioned as "step 1 of the program," its price **credited toward the flagship** on upgrade.

## Decision B — Price point

**Choice: flagship 109 000 ₽ · mini-course 19 900 ₽.**

Market clusters 93–120k (street), floor ~110k, ceiling 215k sticker (§8). No brand pulls price *down*; AI moat pulls it *up* → land **mid-market, not premium, at launch.** Premium comes after hired-alumni proof.

| Price | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| ~79–90k | Easy sell; undercuts all | Signals low quality; margin dangerous after partner + AI costs; hard to raise later |
| **~99–129k (chosen 109k)** | Credible vs Skillbox/Netology sticker; AI moat justifies; survives partner cut | Requires AI features visible in the pitch |
| 150k+ | Quality signal; fat margin | Indefensible with zero brand/alumni at launch |

19 900 ₽ mini-course **undercuts systemanalyst.life's ~30k cheapest unit** → easiest first yes in the market.

## Decision C — Installment (HALAL constraint)

**Choice: self-financed halal installment framed as ijarah — fixed price, no interest, no late fees. Certified Islamic fintech for longer terms. Never an interest-bearing bank.**

> **Why the earlier "bank рассрочка" recommendation is dropped:** a conventional bank that lends the buyer money at interest (or quotes a time-based rate) is **riba — haram**. Even "0%" bank rails are structure-dependent and live in the ambiguous zone scholars warn about. This decision is now governed by the fiqh research → see §Shariah Compliance + §Halal Installment Structure.

§8's market truth still holds: **buyers compare the monthly number, not the lump sum.** The halal news: Islam **permits charging a higher price for deferred payment** (bay' bi-thaman ajil / ijarah) — so you can still offer an attractive monthly figure *and* price in the time value, as long as it's a fixed sale price (not interest) locked at signing.

| Approach | Pros | Cons | Halal? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lump sum only | Simple; full cash upfront | Loses to competitors' monthly framing; sticker shock | ✅ |
| Interest-bearing bank рассрочка | Full cash upfront; bank eats default risk | **Riba** | 🚩 **HARAM — rejected** |
| **Self-financed ijarah installment (chosen, core)** | Halal; full control; access-gating gives strong leverage | You carry cash-timing + default risk | ✅ |
| Certified Islamic fintech / murabaha (chosen, scale path) | Halal; offloads cash-timing for longer terms | Needs a Shariah-board-certified provider; provider fee | ✅ (if genuinely certified) |

A solo founder normally shouldn't be a lender — but a **course is the ideal self-financed asset**: you can **suspend access the moment a payment is missed**, which is enforcement leverage a car loan never has. That changes the risk math and makes self-financing viable here. See the structure + cash-flow defenses below.

## Decision D — Job guarantee

**Choice: NO cash guarantee at launch. Conditional, non-cash "outcome commitment."**

You have no hiring partners / no alumni (your listed weakness). A cash guarantee you can't back is a **solvency landmine**: refunds hit *after* the partner is already paid → you eat 100% of a sale you kept only 70% of.

| Option | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| Hard "work or money back" | Strongest pitch; matches Skillbox/GeekBrains | Catastrophic refund liability w/o hiring partners; you can't control the job market |
| No guarantee, support only | Zero refund risk; matches OTUS/Yandex | Weaker pitch for an unknown brand |
| **Conditional non-cash (chosen)** | Most of the sales benefit; caps liability; conditions filter non-serious students | Must be worded carefully or reads weasel-y |

**Wording:** *"Complete the program and apply to 30+ roles — not hired in 3 months? Free continued coaching + mock interviews until you are."* Costs only your time, not refunds. Upgrade to a real cash guarantee at cohort 3+.

**Choice: "Cohort 0" at 59 000 ₽ × 10–15 founding students.**

First cohort buys **proof, not profit** — testimonials, a case study, feedback. This is a one-time exercise; never anchor your public price here.

## Shariah Compliance (governs all pricing)

> Research summary, not a fatwa. Have a qualified scholar (مفتي) approve the final contract wording before launch. Sources in §Sources.

**The core permission:** Islam permits **selling on credit at a fixed deferred price** (bay' bi-thaman ajil for goods; **ijarah** — sale of usufruct/manfa'ah — for a service like a course). You *may* set the installment price higher than the cash price. This is **not riba**, because the increase attaches to the **price of the service**, not to lent money. (IIFA Res. 51; majority of the four schools.)

**The line that turns it haram — riba al-nasi'ah:**
- Any charge that **increases the debt for late payment** (late fee, penalty-to-you, "cost of funds").
- Quoting an explicit **interest rate** as a time-based line item separate from the price.
- A **bank lending the buyer money at interest** to pay you — or you being a party to that interest contract.
- Raising the price **after** a sale was already concluded, in exchange for more time.
- Leaving the contract **ambiguous between two prices** ("two sales in one").

### ✅ HALAL checklist (the plan must meet ALL)
- [ ] Buyer locks **one** price at signing — cash OR deferred — no open ambiguity.
- [ ] Total price, installment count, due dates **fixed and known upfront**.
- [ ] Any markup **baked into the price**, never quoted as an interest rate.
- [ ] Price **never rises** for late payment — not by prior agreement, not after.
- [ ] You actually **own/control** the platform and can deliver access (the manfa'ah).
- [ ] Default handled only by halal means (below).
- [ ] **No interest-bearing loan** anywhere in the chain.
- [ ] Early-payment discount, if any, is **voluntary** — not a promised contractual right.

## Halal Installment Structure (replaces bank рассрочка)

**Frame the contract as ijarah** — the student pays for access to the educational service over time. Concretely:

1. **Offer two clean prices; buyer picks one at signing.**
   - **Cash:** 109 000 ₽ (or the Cohort-0 / promo price).
   - **Deferred (installment):** a *higher fixed total*, e.g. ~119 000–129 000 ₽, locked in writing — halal time-value, not interest.
2. **Headline the monthly number** (deferred total ÷ term) — matches market framing without quoting a rate.
3. **Meaningful down payment** (e.g. 25–40%) — cuts your cash-timing strain and default exposure immediately.
4. **Align the term with the program** (~8–10 months), not 36 — a solo founder self-financing 3 years is reckless.
5. **No late fees, ever.** The price is the price.

### Halal default enforcement (your leverage — all permitted by AAOIFI SS No. 3 / IIFA)
- **Suspend course access** on a missed payment — the strongest, cleanest tool: it's a *service*, so withholding the manfa'ah needs no penalty. This is why self-financing works here.
- **Acceleration clause** — a *solvent* defaulter's remaining (fixed) installments fall due at once. Notify + give ~2 weeks first. Adds no money — just calls in the existing debt.
- **Charity-penalty** — contract may oblige a defaulter to donate a set sum to **charity** (deterrent). **Not one ruble may reach you** or it becomes riba.
- **Guarantor (kafala) / collateral (rahn)** — optional, for higher-ticket buyers.

### Who carries the financing
- **Default / core path: you self-finance** the ~8–10-month plan. Viable because access-gating + down payment cap the risk.
- **Scale path: a *certified* Islamic fintech / murabaha provider** for buyers wanting longer terms — only with a real Shariah supervisory board. Offloads cash-timing. Never a conventional interest "0%" rail.

## Unit Economics Model

> The numbers that decide whether the partner ratchet holds. All per-student, flagship at 109 000 ₽.

### Assumptions (tune these as real data arrives)

| Line | Estimate | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Flagship price (gross) | 109 000 ₽ cash · ~119–129k deferred | deferred = halal fixed time-value, not interest |
| Payment processing | ~3% | card/processor fee on a sale (not a loan) — halal |
| Default / cash-timing reserve | ~3–5% of self-financed revenue | replaces the (haram) bank that used to absorb this; mitigated by down payment + access-gating |
| Founder COGS per student | **~16 000 ₽** | AI/API ~8k (chatbot, grading, simulation, voice) + live/curator ~6k + processing/reserve ~2k |
| Partner share | 35% / 30% / 25% | ratchet |
| CAC (funded by partner from their share) | 10 000–25 000 ₽ | Russian EdTech, ~100k course |

### Founder net margin per student (after own COGS)

| Partner split | Founder gross share | − COGS 16k | **Founder net** |
|---|---|---|---|
| 35% | 70 850 ₽ | −16 000 | **54 850 ₽** |
| 30% | 76 300 ₽ | −16 000 | **60 300 ₽** |
| 25% | 81 750 ₽ | −16 000 | **65 750 ₽** |

Founder is healthy at every split. The constraint is the **partner side**:

### Partner net per student (their share − CAC) — this is what makes/breaks the ratchet

| | CAC 25k | CAC 20k | CAC 15k | CAC 10k |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **35%** (38 150 ₽) | 13 150 | 18 150 | 23 150 | 28 150 |
| **30%** (32 700 ₽) | 7 700 | 12 700 | 17 700 | 22 700 |
| **25%** (27 250 ₽) | **2 250** | 7 250 | 12 250 | 17 250 |

**Read the bottom-left corner.** At 25% with high CAC (25k), the partner nets **2 250 ₽/sale** — they will quit. This is the proof that the ratchet **must step down on falling CAC, not on student count.** Cut the split before the brand lowers CAC → you starve the partner before they can survive on it.

### Ratchet triggers (CAC-based)

| Stage | Condition | Partner split |
|---|---|---|
| Launch | No brand, CAC high | **35%** |
| Growth | Blended CAC < ~15 000 ₽ | **30%** |
| Scale | Referral/organic dominate, CAC low | **25%** |

### Front-door mini-course (19 900 ₽) — tripwire, not profit center

Partner 35% = ~6 965 ₽; low absolute ₽ means **don't expect ads to pay back on the mini-course alone.** Its job: cheap first yes → email list → upsell to flagship. Judge it on **flagship conversion rate of mini-course buyers**, not its own margin.

### Cohort 0 (59 000 ₽) — proof, not margin

After COGS ~16k and any partner share, near-breakeven by design. The return is testimonials + case study, which unlock the 109k price.

## Open decisions to validate with Cohort 0

1. **Real CAC** — the single most important unknown; it sets the entire ratchet schedule.
2. **AI/API cost per student** — heavy AI usage (voice mock interview, grading) could exceed the 8k estimate; meter it.
3. **Mini-course → flagship conversion rate** — decides if the hybrid front-door works.
4. **Self-financed default rate** — how many installment buyers stop paying; access-gating should keep it low. Sets the reserve %.
5. **Cash vs deferred mix** — what share choose installments; determines your cash-timing strain.
6. **Refund rate under the conditional commitment** — confirms the non-cash guarantee stays cheap.
7. **Scholar sign-off** — final contract wording approved by a qualified mufti before launch.
