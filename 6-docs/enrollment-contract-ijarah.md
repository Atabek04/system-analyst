# Student Enrollment Agreement (Ijarah) — Draft

> ⚠️ Template, not a fatwa or legal advice. Have a qualified scholar (مفتي) **and** a lawyer approve before use. Fiqh basis: [pricing-strategy.md §Shariah Compliance](pricing-strategy.md).

```
STUDENT ENROLLMENT AGREEMENT
(Ijarah — provision of educational service for a fixed price)

Parties:
  (1) [PROVIDER NAME / ENTITY] — "Provider"
  (2) [STUDENT FULL NAME] — "Student"
Date: __________

1. SERVICE
   Provider grants Student access to the "[COURSE NAME]" program:
   recorded lessons, live sessions, assessments, AI tools, and curator
   support, for the program duration of [N] months from the start date.

2. PRICE — STUDENT SELECTS ONE OPTION AT SIGNING (final, no ambiguity)
   ☐ Option A — Cash:        109 000 ₽, paid in full before access.
   ☐ Option B — Installment: [e.g. 125 000 ₽] total, FIXED, paid as:
       - Down payment: [e.g. 40 000 ₽] before access begins;
       - then [e.g. 8] monthly payments of [e.g. 10 625 ₽],
         due on the [__] of each month.
   The total price chosen is fixed and final. It does NOT change for
   any reason, including late payment. (No interest; no late fees.)

3. NATURE OF PRICE
   The installment total is a fixed sale price for deferred payment,
   not a loan and not interest. No financing rate applies.

4. ACCESS
   Access begins once the cash payment / down payment is received.
   Access continues while payments are current.

5. LATE OR MISSED PAYMENT (no monetary penalty to Provider)
   If a payment is missed:
   (a) Provider may SUSPEND access until the overdue amount is paid;
   (b) For a solvent Student who delays without valid excuse, all
       remaining installments become due at once (acceleration), after
       written notice and a [14]-day cure period;
   (c) Student agrees to donate [SUM] ₽ to a registered charity for
       unexcused delay. This sum is paid to charity, NOT to Provider,
       and forms no part of Provider's income.
   No amount is ever added to the price for late payment.

6. EARLY PAYMENT
   Student may settle early. Any discount for early settlement is at
   Provider's sole discretion and is not a promised right.

7. REFUNDS / OUTCOME COMMITMENT
   [Refund window: e.g. 14 days from start.]
   Outcome commitment (non-cash): if Student completes the program and
   applies to 30+ relevant roles but is not hired within 3 months,
   Provider continues coaching and mock interviews at no extra cost
   until Student is hired. No cash guarantee.

8. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
   All course content, materials, and access rights remain Provider's
   property. Student receives a personal, non-transferable right to use
   the materials for learning only; no copying, resale, or sharing.

9. TERMINATION
   Either party may terminate for material breach not cured within
   [14] days of written notice. On termination, access ends; amounts
   for the service already provided remain due per the fixed schedule.

10. GOVERNING LAW
   [JURISDICTION]. Disputes resolved by [courts / arbitration].

SIGNED:
  Provider: ______________________  Date: __________
  Student:  ______________________  Date: __________
```

## Halal-compliance check (every box must hold)
- [ ] One price locked at signing (A or B) — no open ambiguity.
- [ ] Installment total fixed; never rises for delay.
- [ ] No interest rate quoted anywhere.
- [ ] Default handled only by: suspend access · acceleration · charity-penalty (not to Provider).
- [ ] Provider owns/delivers the service (manfa'ah).
- [ ] Early-payment discount voluntary, not a promised right.
