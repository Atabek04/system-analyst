## **MODULE 2: LESSON STRUCTURE & INTERACTION**

### Slide 3: "The 90-Minute Energy Map"

**Key Points:**

- 0-15 min: Hook + context (why this matters)
- 15-40 min: Core content with Q&A breaks
- 40-45 min: **Stand up break** (literally)
- 45-70 min: Live exercise/pair work
- 70-90 min: Wrap-up + homework brief

### Slide 4: "Question Techniques We Use"

**Key Points:**

- **Cold calling** (random name generator on screen)
- **Think-Pair-Share**: 1 min alone → 2 min pairs → class discussion
- **Fist to Five**: Show fingers for confidence level (0-5)
- **Parking Lot**: Post-it notes for "ask later" questions

### Slide 5: "When You See Zombies (Attention Drop Signals)"

**Key Points:**

- Phones appearing = time to switch
- Silence to questions = too theoretical
- Side conversations = lost the thread
- **Our responses:** Mini-quiz, stand-up activity, change topic order

### Slide 6: "Attention Reset Techniques"

**Key Points:**

- **2-Minute Challenge**: "Draw the current system on paper NOW"
- **Pair Debug**: "Find 3 mistakes in this requirements doc"
- **Stand-up Modeling**: Use people as database tables
- **Kahoot Lightning Round**: 5 questions, leaderboard

## **MODULE 3: LIVE EXERCISES & PAIR WORK**

### Slide 7: "Pair Work Rules"

**Key Points:**

- Navigator-Driver rotation (swap every 10 min)
- Both names on submission
- Different partner each session
- Teach-back requirement (explain to partner)

### Slide 8: "Live Exercise Types"

**Key Points:**

- **Whiteboard Race**: Teams diagram same requirement
- **Requirements Auction**: Bid time/cost on user stories
- **SQL Battle**: Who writes better query?
- **Stakeholder Roleplay**: One is BA, one is angry customer

## **MODULE 5: SPACED REPETITION & RETENTION**

### Slide 14: "The Forgetting Curve Problem"

**Key Points:**

- Show Ebbinghaus curve graph
- After 1 day: 60% forgotten (without review)
- After 1 week: 90% forgotten
- Solution: Spaced repetition = 10% forgotten

### Slide 15: "Anki Investment Proposition"

**Key Points:**

- iOS app costs $25 (one-time)
- Desktop/Android = FREE
- **Your offer**: Course is free, Anki is your textbook cost
- Alternative: AnkiWeb (free but less convenient)
- Show your personal Anki stats (proof it works)

### Slide 16: "Free Alternatives (If Really Can't Buy)"

**Key Points:**

- **Quizlet** (free, but ads + weaker algorithm)
- **RemNote** (free, outliner + flashcards)
- **Obsidian + Spaced Repetition Plugin** (free, nerdy)
- **Our stance**: Strongly recommend Anki, but won't force

### Slide 17: "Flashcard Design Principles"

**Key Points:**

- **Atomic**: 1 card = 1 concept
- **BAD**: "Explain normalization forms"
- **GOOD**: "3NF eliminates: ____ (transitive dependencies)"
- Include context, avoid yes/no
- Show 5 examples (good vs bad pairs)

### Slide 18: "Weekly Review Ritual"

**Key Points:**

- **Sunday Session**: Review all cards due (20-30 min)
- **Daily maintenance**: 5 min before breakfast
- Create cards **immediately after class** (fresh)
- Shared deck available, but make your own too

### Slide 19: "Kahoot Optimization & Prize Psychology"

**Key Points:**

- Every session starts with 5-question review Kahoot
- Top 3 get **small prizes** (chocolate, coffee voucher)
- Questions = previous week content ONLY
- Leaderboard tracked across all sessions
- Semester winner: **Big prize** (book, course cert, recommendation letter)

### Slide 20: "Gamification Without Cringe"

**Key Points:**

- We're not kindergarten → no stickers
- Competition = optional (introverts can opt out)
- Real reward = knowledge + portfolio
- Prizes = just fun bonus, not main motivation

## **MODULE 6: ENERGY & ATTENTION MANAGEMENT**

### Slide 21: "Reading the Room"

**Key Points:**

- **Green flags**: Questions, eye contact, note-taking
- **Yellow flags**: Silence, checking time, yawning
- **Red flags**: Phones out, side conversations, asking "how much longer?"
- Your response protocol for each

### Slide 22: "Break Types & When to Use"

**Key Points:**

- **Micro-break** (2 min): Stand, stretch, window check
- **Activity break** (5 min): Walk around office, bathroom, coffee
- **Switch break** (0 min): Change from lecture to exercise
- Rule: Never go 45+ min without one of these

### Slide 23: "Emergency Engagement Techniques"

**Key Points:**

- **When all else fails**:
    1. "Everyone stand up NOW" → partner discussion standing
    2. Show funny relevant meme → discuss why it's accurate
    3. Tell war story from real job
    4. Early break + come back to problem-solving

## **MODULE 8: COMMON PITFALLS & SOLUTIONS**

### Slide 27: "When Students Don't Do Flashcards"

**Key Points:**

- **Diagnosis**: Kahoot scores drop across everyone
- **Solution 1**: Make 5 cards together in class
- **Solution 2**: Share your personal deck as example
- **Solution 3**: Dedicate 10 min to card creation in class

### Slide 28: "When Pair Work Fails"

**Key Points:**

- One person dominates → forced role swap mid-exercise
- Both silent → give them more structured task breakdown
- Personality clash → allow re-pairing once per course

### Slide 29: "When Portfolio Work is Garbage"

**Key Points:**

- Set minimum quality bar (show examples)
- **Harsh love**: "This won't get you hired" (private feedback)
- Offer 1-on-1 portfolio clinic (optional, outside class)
- Show before/after transformations
